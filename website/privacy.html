Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

poco start (“we”, “us”) is a Spanish learning app for beginners, available on iOS and Android, with information at pocostart.com. This policy explains what data we process and why. Contact: hello@pocostart.com.

1. Data we process

  • Account: email address, display name, and sign-in identifiers if you create an account (email/password or Sign in with Apple).
  • Learning progress: XP, streak, completed lessons, and preferred app language.
  • Subscriptions: purchase and entitlement status handled by Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat (we do not store your full payment card details).
  • Device permissions (only when you use the feature): microphone and speech recognition for pronunciation practice; photo library if you choose to set a profile picture (where available).
  • Guest mode: you may try the app without an account; progress may stay on your device until you sign in.

2. Why we process data

  • Provide the app, sync progress, and personalize language.
  • Authenticate you and secure your account.
  • Manage subscriptions and unlock Pro features.
  • Improve pronunciation practice when you use Speak.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Service providers

We use trusted processors to run the product:

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud storage of account/progress data.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status across App Store and Google Play.
  • Apple / Google — app distribution, payments, Sign in with Apple (if used), and on-device speech features where applicable.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies, and only as needed to provide their services to us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the providers above, or if required by law, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

We keep account and progress data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account, or ask us by emailing hello@pocostart.com. Purchase records may be retained as required by Apple, Google, or tax/accounting rules. Cancel subscriptions separately in the App Store or Google Play.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted transport and access controls via our providers). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children

poco start is a general audience education app. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) without appropriate consent. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including EEA/UK/Norway), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object or restrict certain processing. Email hello@pocostart.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the EU/EEA or other countries where our providers operate. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
